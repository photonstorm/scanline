# The app's icons

45 of the icons in this folder are from **Streamline Core Line – Free**, by Streamline (https://streamlinehq.com), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

They were exported white, at Streamline's own exporter, and renamed for what they do in the app; their drawings are unchanged. Each file keeps Streamline's note naming it. Streamline's free licence allows up to 50 of its free icons in one project: keep this folder within that.
