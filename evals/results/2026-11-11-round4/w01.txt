EVIDENCE: Capture cap_1 had 23 draws. Draws 6, 7 and 8 use pip_1 «solid» with tex_2 «crate» and each reported "changed nothing". draw 6 said that with face culling off it would change 6400 pixels (80x80), and that every triangle is culled (cull back, front face cw). The ground draw 5, which uses the same pipeline, has rect 0,260,480,100 (positive width) and renders. The minimap yellow-dot draws and the glass panel render fine.
FIX: Pass positive widths for the crate rects, so the rect is the left edge plus the width, for example 60,180,80,80 for the first crate, with the same left edge and size convention as the ground. If the negative width was an intended horizontal flip, do the flip in UVs or turn culling off (cullMode none) for the sprite pipeline. Do not flip the winding by using a negative size.
