CAUSE: The page enables the scissor test with a box of 12,336,150,12 (the health bar) to draw the HUD, and never disables it (or resets it to the full canvas). On the next frame the scissor is still on, so the clear and every draw are clipped to that strip.

EVIDENCE: In a capture of frame 106 (cap_5), draw_list shows "scissor 12, 336, 150, 12" on every draw and on the offscreen pass, from event 1 through 24. In the first frame (cap_1, taken by another evaluator but visible to me), only draw 23 (the health bar) had that scissor. pixel_source at (240,200) shows the pixel was produced by event 4, the canvas clear, with final value (0,0,0,255). Nothing else changed it, because the scissor rejected every later draw there. The strip at the bottom of the 150x12 box is the only part still drawn. (I did not verify which screen corner the strip appears at.)

FIX: Call gl.disable(gl.SCISSOR_TEST) right after the health-bar draw. Alternatively, enable the scissor before that draw and disable it after. Or reset the scissor to the full canvas at the start of each frame, before the clears.
