CAUSE: The page renders the minimap into a 64x64 offscreen texture (tex_3) with gl.viewport(0,0,64,64), then draws the main scene to the 480x360 canvas without resetting the viewport. The 64x64 viewport persists, so the whole scene is squashed into a 64x64 area at the bottom-left of the canvas.
EVIDENCE: In cap_9, the minimap pass (draws 0-3 → tex_3, 64x64) has viewport 0,0,64,64, which is correct. The canvas pass (clear at event 4, draws 5-24) also has viewport 0,0,64,64 (query on draws.viewport for draws 4, 5 and 24). The uniform resolution is [480,360] and the rects are in 480x360 pixel units (for example rect [80,120,40,40]), so the canvas viewport should be 0,0,480,360. Draw 24, which blits the minimap texture to the canvas, has the same stale viewport.
FIX: After the minimap render-target pass, and before drawing to the canvas (rebinding framebuffer null), call gl.viewport(0, 0, canvas.width, canvas.height), i.e. 480x360. Setting the viewport every time a framebuffer is bound avoids the stale-state bug.
