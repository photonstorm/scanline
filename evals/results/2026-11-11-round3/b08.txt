CAUSE: The page never binds the crate texture (tex_1, 64x64 mipmapped, made by makeCrateTexture) before drawing the crates. The only texture bound in the whole frame is tex_2, the 32x1 health-bar texture, so the crates are sampled from the bar texture.

EVIDENCE:
- cap_12 draw_list: draws 6, 7 and 8 are the crates (rects 60,180,80,80; 200,160,100,100; 340,190,70,70). All use prg_1 with "textures tex_2".
- `draw cap_12 6` shows unit 0 holds tex_2 (32x1 RGBA8), "last bound at call 2, for event 0; not bound again for this draw". The uniforms are textured=1 and tint=1,1,1,1, so the shader does sample it.
- `resources texture` lists tex_1 (64x64, 7 levels, makeCrateTexture at scene.js:154). It has "used f0" only, so it is not used after frame 0.
- The commands table for the frame has exactly one bindTexture call, at call 2 with tex_2. There is no bindTexture of tex_1 before draws 6-8.
- The same tex_2 is bound for the ground, glass, sparks and health bar. Only the minimap draw 24 uses a different texture, tex_3 (the render target).

FIX: Before the crate draws (draws 6, 7 and 8), call gl.bindTexture(gl.TEXTURE_2D, crateTexture) with the texture from makeCrateTexture (tex_1). Then bind tex_2 again before the health-bar draws. Alternatively, bind the right texture per draw group.
