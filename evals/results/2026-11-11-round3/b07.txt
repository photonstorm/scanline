CAUSE: The page enables SCISSOR_TEST with a 12,336,150,12 box for the health bar fill and never disables it. The scissor stays on into the next frame, so every clear and draw (scene pass, offscreen pass, minimap) is clipped to that strip and the rest of the canvas is left black.

EVIDENCE: In capture cap_7 (frame 201), draw_list shows scissor ON 12,336,150,12 on all 25 events, including the clears (events 0 and 4, "frame began with scissor already on") and the scene draws 5 to 21. Those draws "changed nothing", and only the clear and draws 22 and 23 changed 1800 px at 12,12,150,12. The capture's own "worth a look" note says the scissor test was already on at frame start and call 138 switches it on with nothing after it switching it off. The commands table shows gl.enable SCISSOR_TEST at call 138 and gl.scissor(12,336,150,12) at call 139, just before the health bar draw (call 145). Nothing in calls 138 to 151 disables it. The minimap draw 24 (tex_3, 406,10,64,64) also changed nothing because it is clipped. The offscreen pass (tex_3) is clipped too, so the minimap texture stays empty.

FIX: After the health bar draw, call gl.disable(gl.SCISSOR_TEST). Alternatively, enable the scissor only around that draw and disable it afterwards. Also disable it, or reset it, at the start of each frame, before the clears.
