CAUSE: The glass panel is drawn with gl.BLEND disabled, so its tint alpha of 0.4 is ignored and the draw overwrites the crates behind it. The page enables blending only for the spark draws (prg_2) and never for the panel draw.
EVIDENCE: In capture cap_4, event 9 is a TRIANGLE_STRIP x4 on prg_1 to the canvas. It has uniform rect = 150,120,220,160, textured = 0 and tint = 0.2,0.4,1,0.4, and depth LESS with no depth write. The `draw` output says "blend: off (the draw replaces what is there)", and it changed 35200 pixels over x=150..370, y=120..280, which is the whole panel rectangle. The capture's "Worth a look" note says the same: "Event 9 is drawn with blending off, but its uniform tint has an alpha of 0.4". Draw_list shows blend SRC_ALPHA/ONE only on events 10-21 (the sparks, prg_2). The crates (events 6-8) are drawn before event 9 and are covered by it. I did not verify the fix: my one patch_state attempt used an invalid filter key ("draw") and was rejected, and the screenshot after it came from a frame with no patch in force.
FIX: Before the panel draw (event 9), call gl.enable(gl.BLEND) and gl.blendFunc(gl.SRC_ALPHA, gl.ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA). Disable blending again afterwards if the draws after it (events 10-21) need to set their own state. The sparks already set SRC_ALPHA/ONE themselves.
