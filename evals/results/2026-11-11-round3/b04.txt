CAUSE: The ground draw (an untextured, flat-colour draw, textured=0) never sets the `tint` uniform, so prg_1 keeps the stale value left by the last minimap-dot draw, (1, 0.8, 0.2, 1) yellow-orange. The page forgot to upload the green colour to `tint` before drawing the ground.
