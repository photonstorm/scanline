CAUSE: Every frame the minimap pass (renderMap) calls makeMapTarget, which creates a new render-target texture, and the texture is never deleted or reused. GPU memory grows by about 250 KB per frame.
EVIDENCE: memory() after 30 frames reported "LIKELY LEAK: 30 textures still alive (7.5 MB) were all created by one line, over frames 1 to 30 (about 1 a frame): makeMapTarget (fixtures/bugs/scene.js:159:28) < renderMap (scene.js:227:26) < frame (scene.js:367:9)". resources() showed 33 textures alive and 1 framebuffer, with 7.8 MB of render targets. The page reported no errors.
FIX: Create the minimap target once, outside the per-frame loop, and reuse it each frame (re-attach it to the framebuffer, or cache it). Alternatively call gl.deleteTexture on the old target (and its framebuffer, if one is made each time) at the end of renderMap. Do this inside makeMapTarget or renderMap at scene.js:159 and 227.
